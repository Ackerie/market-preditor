Delete your NexusTrade account

You can permanently request deletion of your NexusTrade account and its associated application data. This does not close your external broker accounts or cancel orders already submitted to a broker.

Delete from the app

  1. Sign in to the NexusTrade app or website.
  2. Open Account, then Profile.
  3. Choose Delete account, enter your password, and confirm deletion.

Deletion removes the account and associated credentials, settings, and application records from the active production database. Some information may be retained where required by law or in protected backups until those backups expire.

Can't sign in?

Email trivisionja@gmail.com with the subject “NexusTrade account deletion”. Do not email your password, broker API keys, or AI provider keys. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing your request.

Before deleting

  • Disable automated trading and review any pending broker activity.
  • Revoke broker and AI-provider API keys with those providers.
  • Contact your broker directly about open positions or orders.
