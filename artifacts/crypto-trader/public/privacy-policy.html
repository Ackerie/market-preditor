Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This policy describes how NexusTrade handles information when you use the NexusTrade website or Android app. NexusTrade provides market information, analysis tools, and controls for broker accounts that you choose to connect. NexusTrade is not a broker, bank, or investment adviser.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, an optional first and last name, a generated account identifier, and a password-derived hash. We do not store your password in plaintext.
  • Trading and preference information: broker connection details and credentials you choose to provide, any previously saved personal AI-provider keys, your monthly shared-AI spending limit and usage estimates, watchlists, trading and automation settings, order-related requests, analysis activity, and application history associated with your account.
  • Session and technical information: session tokens needed to authenticate you, security-related request information, and essential browser preferences. Android session tokens are stored in the device's secure storage.
  • Support communications: information you include when you contact us.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide the requested market and account features, connect to supported brokers and our shared AI providers, process your trading instructions, enforce the monthly AI limit you set, operate automation you enable, respond to support requests, send requested password-reset and account alert emails, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Broker credentials and previously saved personal AI-provider keys are encrypted before storage on our server. Personal AI keys are no longer used for requests; shared provider keys are managed server-side. Passwords are stored as derived password hashes. Information sent between the app and our services uses HTTPS. No security method is perfect, and you are responsible for protecting your device and account.

When information is shared

We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising. We share or transmit information only as needed to provide requested features, protect the service, or comply with law. This can include:

  • Your selected broker: when you connect a brokerage account, requests and credentials needed to retrieve account data or submit orders are sent to that broker. Orders are subject to the broker's terms and execution policies.
  • Our AI providers: when you request AI analysis, prompts and relevant market or account context may be sent to Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google, depending on the feature. Your broker credentials and AI API keys are not included in those prompts. We record provider-reported token usage to estimate consumption against your monthly limit. Providers handle submitted content under their own policies and settings.
  • Service providers: our hosting, database, email, market-data, and content-delivery providers process information needed to run the service. Loading web fonts from Google may also disclose ordinary connection information such as your IP address to Google.
  • Legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, or address fraud, security, or service-integrity issues.

Cookies and local storage

The website uses essential authentication and CSRF cookies and stores limited interface preferences in browser storage. The Android app uses encrypted secure storage for its session token. We do not currently use advertising cookies or cross-site advertising trackers.

Retention and account deletion

We retain account information while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service. You can request deletion from the app's Account > Profile area, or follow the instructions at NexusTrade account deletion. Deleting an account removes its active account, stored credentials, settings, and associated application records from the production database, subject to legal retention requirements and the ordinary expiration of protected backups. It does not cancel or reverse orders already sent to a broker, close positions, or revoke credentials at a broker or AI provider. Revoke those credentials with the provider separately.

Children and eligibility

NexusTrade is intended only for people who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review and delete it.

Your choices and requests

You may update optional account preferences, remove saved AI-provider keys, disconnect broker connections, and request account deletion through the app. You may also contact us with questions or privacy requests. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Changes and contact
